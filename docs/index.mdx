Prism gives coding agents local context before an edit and checks the change
after it. Prepare says what to open. Envelope shows what Prism put into the
agent's context and what it cost. The receipt runs the checks a change reaches
and says whether it is proven, unproven, or failed. Analysis has no model, and
your agent makes the edits.
