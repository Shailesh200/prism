---
title: Introduction
description: "What Prism does before and after your agent edits, and where to start."
---

Prism gives coding agents local context before an edit and checks the change
after it. A review ranks a change by risk and runs the lens checks you pick.
When
the agent stops, the receipt runs the checks the change reaches and gives a
verdict: SHIP, NOT PROVEN or NO-SHIP. Analysis has no model, and your agent
makes the edits.
