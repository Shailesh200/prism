  </Tab>

## What you get back

**Health score** (0–100, higher better) from five factors: parse health, test
presence, coupling, modularity, diagnostics. The factors matter more than the
letter grade.

**Engineering report** — entropy, drift, debt, churn, hotspots (high churn ×
high complexity).

**Trends** — snapshots keyed by commit; early points may be git-backfilled and
marked estimated. See [signal provenance](/docs/concepts/signal-provenance).

## When this is wrong

- Not a code-quality or correctness score — structural heuristics only.
- Not comparable across repositories; compare a repo to its own past.
- Unavailable coverage/churn is absence, not zero — do not treat missing as
  green.
