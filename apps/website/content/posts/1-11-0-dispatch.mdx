Dispatch keeps every job in one global store. Wake and Spectrum share the same status screen.

- **Jobs** live under `~/.prism` (ADR-0054). Switching checkouts no longer drops rows. A leftover `{repo}/.prism/dispatch/jobs.json` is lifted after the global file is written.
- **Wake** shows Dispatch and Spectrum separately. Close or Escape leaves the page without re-indexing.
- **Spectrum** follows the repository in the URL. A background poll cannot snap you off a checkout you picked.
- Footer DNA/Health links pass that repo. `prism wake` / `prism sleep` speak Spectrum, not Playground.

Keep `@latest`. Reload MCP after this publish. See [Prism Dispatch](/docs/guides/dispatch).
