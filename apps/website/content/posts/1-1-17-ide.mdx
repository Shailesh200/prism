title: "IDE — Console, jobs, no Prism sign-in"
description: "The VS Code and Cursor extension opens the local Console. Host connectors stay in the editor. Nothing is merged for you."
version: "1.1.17"
---

The extension is a window onto the same engine, not a second product.

- Open the Console from the sidebar: Jobs, Workflows, and Host Connectors.
- There is no Prism account and no connect flow in the panel. Connectors belong to Cursor or VS Code.
- A finished checkout job leaves edits uncommitted in your tree. A worktree job is ready on its branch. You review; Prism does not merge or push.

Install from the marketplace as **Prism** (`prismhq.repo-prism`), or follow the [Install](/docs/start/install) IDE tab.
