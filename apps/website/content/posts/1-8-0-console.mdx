The Dispatch Console is a fleet home, not a reading column of job cards.

- **Pulse** is the default and the inbox: Live / Needs you / Settled, with wait and work as two meters. Open a card for Focus — billed in/out lives there.
- **Focus** draws an append-only job graph (Accepted → Queued → Working). Pause then resume adds a new Working node; the rail never rewinds.
- **Board** is one tile per repository. **List** is the sortable table. `/` filters all three views.
- **New job** opens compose on the hub. Chat is no longer the only door.
- Related jobs is a parent/child tree with status dots and a You marker.

Open **Prism: Open Agent Dashboard** or `http://prismhq.localhost:17330/`. See [Prism Dispatch](/docs/guides/dispatch).
