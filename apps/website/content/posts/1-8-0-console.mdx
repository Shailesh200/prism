The Dispatch Console is a fleet home, not a reading column of job cards.

- **Pulse** is the default: Live / Needs you / Settled, with wait and work as two meters. Open a card for Focus — billed in/out lives there.
- **Board** is one tile per repository — live count, last job, last verification. Click through to the jobs in range.
- **List** is the sortable table. `/` filters all three views.
- **New job** opens compose on the hub. Chat is no longer the only door.
- **Attention** is the inbox. Findings can hand a brief to a teammate.

Open **Prism: Open Agent Dashboard** or `http://prismhq.localhost:17330/`. See [Prism Dispatch](/docs/guides/dispatch).
