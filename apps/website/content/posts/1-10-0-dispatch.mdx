Dispatch grows a Skills view and a Trees view. Impact in the playground is one workspace instead of three screens.

- **Skills** groups Prism skills in `~/.prism`. Generate writes one from a brief. They are not committed in the repo.
- **Trees** shows the checkout versus linked worktrees and which job owns each.
- **Impact** is Explain / Blast radius / Review changes on a shared target. Use dirty files on every tab.
- Trends commit charts have real axes and hover.

Keep `@latest`. Reload MCP after this publish. See [Prism Dispatch](/docs/guides/dispatch).
