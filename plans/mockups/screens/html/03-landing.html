How it works

01

Connect your repo

One OAuth tap to GitHub or GitLab. Prism reads your history, branches, and open PRs — never pushes changes back.

Read-only access

What happens under the hood

We parse your AST, resolve cross-file references, and build a directed acyclic graph of your dependency topology. No source code leaves your VPC.

02

Prism analyzes and maps

Within minutes your repository becomes an interactive map with every module, owner, and risk score surfaced.

First map in under 2 min

Signals we compute

Coupling density, change frequency, test coverage drift, and ownership entropy — aggregated into a single health score per module.

03

Explore, share, and act

Pin findings, drop them into Slack threads, and attach blast-radius reports directly to pull requests.

Native Slack & GitHub integrations

Built for team workflows

Shared bookmarks, comment threads on nodes, and one-click PR annotations keep context attached to the work.
